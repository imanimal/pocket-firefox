Motion and feedback

ui/js/motion.js (CA.motion) · the motion section of pocket.css · M2

Every input answers where it was made. Keys travel and spring back, LCDs switch on in steps, pixel icons move a pixel at a time, rows settle into place. Each replay key plays one piece on its specimen, through the same CA.motion the app uses.

Layout

@container screen in pocket.css · regions #top #nav #now #sched #list #bottom

Widths follow the screen container, so an open drawer, split view or Slide Over counts. Heights use the window.
